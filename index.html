I am currently a technical staff in the DC systems, Energy Conversion and Storage group at Technische Universiteit Delft (TU Delft), the Netherlands. Previously, I served as a postdoctoral researcher in the same group, contributing to various national and international projects in collaboration with industry partners.

Before joining TU Delft, I earned my PhD from the Electronics and Electrical Engineering Department at IIT Guwahati, India. My doctoral research focused on enhancing wireless power transfer (WPT) systems for electric vehicle (EV) charging, specifically exploring innovative methods to maximize transmission efficiency and power transfer capability.

My research has spanned a broad range of topics, including integration software development (using S2 standards) for hybrid energy storage systems, design and analysis of off-grid (plug-in) EV chargers, standalone microgrid design and optimization, DC off-grid Vehicle to Grid/Building/Home, and the development of digital twins for electrolyzer systems using Dual Active Bridge (DAB) converter. I have also contributed to analysis and design of the wireless charging coils (with and without ferrites) and power converter development for wireless power transfer (WPT) applications. In addition, I have designed smart solar home systems with distributed charging strategies for rural India.

Currently, my work focuses on energy hubs digital twin with hardware-in-the-loop development for megawatt (MW) charging, integrated magnetics of DAB converters, and long term testing of green hydrogen electrolyzer–fuel cell setups at Green-Village. Through these projects, I aim to drive innovation in sustainable energy and power electronics.

I am always excited to explore new ideas that can make a meaningful impact. I work with industry partners and researchers to create better energy solutions that contribute to a more sustainable future.

PhD Thesis2013 - 2021

Coupling Efficiency Improvement and Power Transfer Enhancement in Wireless Power Transfer System for Electric Vehicle Charging Applications

(key contributions are highlighted below :)

  • Built the experimental setup of SP compensated WPT system with the battery as a load (used Si MOSFETs, high-frequency diodes, Litz wire (handmade), polypropylene capacitors, and FPGA board) and derived analytical expression of the optimum operating frequency to achieve the maximum transmission efficiency throughout the load variation in the charging process (CC and CV modes).
  • Developed a 3-D analytical model for the conventional rectangular (and square) air-core coils (of any dimensions) to calculate the magnetic field densities, self- and mutual inductance, and coupling (k).
  • Invented a novel unipolar coil arrangement method (UCAM) for improving k compared with conventional coils of the same self-inductance and outer dimensions (without flux-guiding materials) in WPT systems (also proposed its analytical model) [Indian patent no. 497323].
  • Proposed a new and simple ferrite arrangement of the rectangular (and square) coil structures (conventional and proposed) to minimize the ferrite volume while maintaining the improvement in k obtained from traditional ferrite arrangement.
